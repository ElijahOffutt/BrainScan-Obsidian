# Quotes & Inspiration

> *"Knowledge exists only in relation."*  
> **— Gregory Bateson**  
> *Knowledge gains meaning through its relationships rather than existing in isolation.*

---

> *"The limits of my language mean the limits of my world."*  
> **— Ludwig Wittgenstein** (*Tractatus Logico-Philosophicus*)  
> *Structured language and representation expand the boundaries of understanding.*

---

> *"We know more than we can tell."*  
> **— Michael Polanyi**  
> *Much human knowledge is tacit; BrainScan seeks to make it explicit and discoverable.*

---

> *"The map is not the territory."*  
> **— Alfred Korzybski**  
> *A knowledge graph is a representation of knowledge, not knowledge itself.*

---

> *"We shape our tools, and thereafter our tools shape us."*  
> **— Marshall McLuhan**  
> *Knowledge management systems influence how we think, learn, and create.*

---

> *"Reality is socially constructed."*  
> **— Peter L. Berger & Thomas Luckmann**  
> *Knowledge emerges through context, interaction, and shared understanding.*

---

> *"To be is to be the value of a variable."*  
> **— W. V. O. Quine**  
> *Ontology begins by defining what exists and how it can be represented.*

---

> *"Information is a difference that makes a difference."*  
> **— Gregory Bateson**  
> *Meaning arises from relationships, distinctions, and context—not isolated facts.*

---

> *"The whole is greater than the sum of its parts."*  
> **— Aristotle**  
> *Understanding emerges from the interconnected structure of knowledge.*

---

> *"Everything should be made as simple as possible, but not simpler."*  
> **— Often attributed to Albert Einstein**  
> *Good knowledge representation balances simplicity with expressive power.*
